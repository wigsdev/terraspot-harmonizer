02 · The Problem

The 2012 step-jump: same fires, 3–8× more hotspots

MODIS (1 km, retiring) built the 2000 baseline. VIIRS (375 m) sees far more raw detections for the same fire. Naively concatenating them fabricates a trend.

Raw monthly hotspots — illustrative

Why it matters

  • Civil defense cannot compare today's fires against the 2000–2011 climate baseline.
  • Agricultural burning is indistinguishable from runaway wildfire in raw counts.
  • MODIS decommissioning makes a sensor-agnostic record urgent.
3–8×

more VIIRS detections vs MODIS, per fire event

03 · Scientific Solution

The TerraSpot 4-Stage Harmonization Engine

Radiometric Masking

Keep nominal/high-confidence vegetation fires (type = 0); drop sunglint, cloud edges and static flares.

0.01° Co-Registration

Collapse pixels into daily Active Fire Cell-Days (AFCD). Discrepancy ~6.5× → ~1.8×.

Seasonal Calibration

Monthly ratio βm over the 2012–2025 overlap. Concordance R² > 0.92.

H(y,m) = β_m · AFCD_MODIS(y,m)

Climatology & Regime

Median baselines, robust Z-scores and a regime index from FRP + night persistence.

Full raw → AFCD → βm → harmonized traceability is preserved at every stage and exported in the 27-column CSV.
